About

A Colorado LLC, one principal, a long NextGen bench.

M&K Consultants, LTD is the healthcare IT practice of Joshua Gross. The firm is registered in Colorado. The work is conversions, Mirth, NextGen, and the occasional custom tool when nothing on the market fits.

Joshua Gross

Joshua has supported NextGen and the products around it for clinics ranging from a solo provider to organizations with more than a thousand. That includes FQHCs, specialty practices, and large public-health settings. The through-line is the same: make the data land, make the interface stay up, and do not leave a mess for the next person.

He is NextGen certified (2025) and ServiceNow certified (2025), based in the Denver / Lakewood area, and works remotely nationwide.
