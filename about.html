About

A Colorado LLC, one principal, Epic demand, a long NextGen bench.

M&K Consultants, LTD is the healthcare IT practice of Joshua Gross. Conversions (especially Epic), Mirth — PDMP, state immunizations, devices — and NextGen from pre-KBM through KBM 8.

Joshua Gross

Joshua has supported NextGen and the products around it for clinics ranging from a solo provider to organizations with more than a thousand. That includes FQHCs, cardiology device clinics, specialty practices, and large public-health settings. The through-line is the same: make the data land in the EHR the organization is actually moving to — often Epic — make the interface stay up, and do not leave a mess for the next person.

He is NextGen certified (2025) and ServiceNow certified (2025), based in the Denver / Lakewood area, and works remotely nationwide.
