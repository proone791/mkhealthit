Services

Move the data. Connect the systems. Keep NextGen honest.

Independent consulting for practices that are mid-conversion, mid-upgrade, or stuck with an interface that almost works. Remote-first, with on-site when go-live needs a body in the room.

EHR conversions

Complete conversions to and from NextGen, Greenway, Allscripts, Amazing Charts, SoapWare, Siemens Signature Gold, and related practice-management systems. The work is mapping what actually lives in production — charts, documents, appointments, charges, and the odd tables vendors forget — then extracting, transforming, and loading it so clinicians still recognize the patient on day one.

Typical engagements include source analysis, field mapping, trial loads, exception lists, and cutover support. Spreadsheet-only conversions are how history disappears. We do not do those.

Mirth Connect / NextGen Connect

Custom channels in JavaScript and SQL. HL7 where it belongs, file and SFTP feeds where that is what the other side will accept, and FHIR when the EHR actually exposes it. Labs, PDMP, billing, pharmacy, appointment reminders, grant extracts, and the vendor that will only take a CSV at 2 a.m.

We write, troubleshoot, and hand off channels you can operate — or we stay on to keep them healthy.

NextGen EHR and EPM

NextGen through v8 and all prior versions, including KBM and EMR upgrades, go-lives, med/CPT/ICD updates, templates, documents, File Maintenance, Blobulizer, Rosetta, BBP, Dragon, DAX, and Nuance. Reporting in SSRS and Crystal. SQL stored procedures, jobs, and SSIS packages.

Reporting, SQL, and ServiceNow

Operational and clinical reports that match how the practice actually works, not how the vendor demo was drawn. ServiceNow support at clinics that already run it. NextGen certified (2025) and ServiceNow certified (2025).
