Featured interface

Pacemaker / ICD · IDCO

Cardiac device interrogations into the EHR

We built a Mirth channel that takes IHE IDCO HL7 from pacemaker and ICD programmers — Biotronik, Medtronic, St. Jude — and lands discrete observations, leads, and a NextGen encounter instead of a PDF in a pile.

Remote vs in-clinic traffic is split, patients are matched, and the chart gets the interrogation the device clinic can actually use. An abnormal or out-of-range reading shows on the pacemaker dashboard and opens a NextGen task to a nursing task group for patient follow-up. See the write-up on Work.
